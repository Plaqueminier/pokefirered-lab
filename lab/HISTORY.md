# History of the lab

The steps of the lab, in order. Each step gives what we added, and what a Run taught us when a Run caused the step. Use it as the plan of a video. Add a step at the end of each piece of work.

## 1. The emulator and the Viewer (2026-10-09 to 10-10)

1. A lab build of FireRed (`make lab`): the same game, plus a direct boot to "Continue" and a fixed RNG seed. The emulator (libmgba) runs in Bun; the browser only shows the game (ADR 0001). `11ebe5b9d`
2. The Viewer: the screen, the keyboard, the speeds, the savestates, and an overlay of the cells (collision, water, ledges, warps, people). `11ebe5b9d`
3. Sound in the Viewer, at ×1 only. `a23150e06`
4. The whole-map view: any map, with its warps, signs, hidden items, triggers and trainer sight lines. `01ebd3a68`

## 2. The Harness and the scripted bot (2026-10-11)

5. The Harness: a Bot calls Macro-actions, and `goTo` finds a path with A*. The first Bot walks to the stairs. `a8eb9af60`
6. `goTo` across maps: doors, stairs, map edges, ledges. The Bot leaves the house. `642647487`
7. Texts and choices: the game freezes at each Decision point (ADR 0002). The Bot follows Oak to his lab. `51cbe9fdb`
8. `interact`, the party and the Milestones: the Bot takes SQUIRTLE. `9d0f47996`
9. The battle Macro-actions: the Bot wins the first rival battle. `a63acf485`
10. The Run record and its replay, the frame budget and the no-progress end. `b8c439379`, `e283d1fbe`
11. Up to 4 Runs at the same time, one shown in the Viewer. `4d3babe25`

## 3. The model bot (2026-10-24 to 10-25)

## 5. What the first long Runs taught us (2026-10-26)

28. A model wrote a stray "{" line before its JSON answer, then copied its error two times: the Run stopped after 3 answers that the Bot could not read. Now the Bot reads the first JSON object of an answer. `bc6f328a8`
29. The Milestone "Get the Pokédex from Professor Oak" was a trap: Oak gives the Pokédex only after the player brings Oak's Parcel from Viridian City. The model talked to Oak for about 140 calls. We removed this Milestone: the model must find the parcel itself. `bc6f328a8`
30. The old no-progress count went back to 0 at each new cell, so it did not show that the model was stuck. A new count: the walk Decision points since a new map, a new Milestone, a level up or a new text of a person. From 50, the prompt tells the model that it is stuck, and the rules tell it to explore. `bc6f328a8`
31. The Nurse of a Pokémon Center stands behind a counter: `go` looked for a free cell next to her and found none, so the model could not heal. Now `go` walks to the front of the counter and talks across it. `bc6f328a8`
32. The Viewer starts with no sound. `bc6f328a8`
33. A Run can continue a stopped Run, also with another model: the Harness replays the buttons of the Run record, keeps its events (the Memory tree, the Mission, the texts of the people), and the model plays on. The cost limit counts the new Model calls only. So a long Run is not lost when it stops, and a cheap model can play the easy parts before a better one. `8de9db896`
34. The Nurse of a Pokémon Center asks YES or NO with a `multichoice` menu, which the Harness did not read: it saw a busy game, and the B presses of the code answered NO. The model could never heal. Now each `multichoice` menu is a choice, and `choose YES` heals. `21f153912`
35. The model asked `goTo 6 10` three times in a house of 10 rows, and `goTo 6 -1 PalletTown` to leave by the north: the reason "no route" did not tell it why. Now the Harness tells when a cell is outside the map, with the cells of the map. After an impossible action, the model bot keeps the conversation and sends the reason and the exits of the map; after 3 in a row, it starts a new prompt. `21f153912`
36. Is the map of the prompt clear for a model? A prototype showed 3 real moments of the Run in 4 layouts: the house, Oak's lab and a Pokémon Center. The layouts were the layout of now, a grid with letters as targets, rows as x ranges, and targets only. 12 blind readers (Claude Opus, Sonnet and Haiku) answered the same questions. All the layouts gave the cells and the nearest target well. But with the layout of now, all three readers said that `goTo 6 10` "is permitted by the syntax": this is the exact error of the Run (`goTo` 6 of 9, exits 7 of 9). The letters grid was the only layout with no mistakes on all three models. The readers also asked for an exits list, the distance and direction of each target, one name and one cell for each person, and marks on the targets used (issue #24).
37. The walk prompt takes the letters grid (issue #24). The grid shows all the rows of the map, with `?` for the cells not seen, `y=` at the start of each row and a legend. The targets are letters (`go B`), so a digit is always a coordinate; `go 2` is now an answer that the Bot cannot read. The extract lists the edges that go to another map, or says that no edge goes to another map. The exits have their own group. In a dead end, as the upstairs of a house, the line "No exit to the outside is seen yet: explore B." points to the unseen cells. The maps one exit away are told from the map of the player ("A (stairs) leads to …"). Each target has its distance and direction ("2 south, 2 east", "adjacent north, you face it") and the marks "talked", "read" and "taken". A person behind a counter says that `go` talks across it. Each person has one cell (where the player last saw it) and one name (the rival has his name everywhere). The past decisions have bullets, not numbers. The model bot is now version 3. `b438f69cb`
38. In the last Run, 5 talks were impossible: `go` walked to the old cell of a person, and the person moved during the walk. Now the first walk goes to the cell of the prompt, then the code reads the cell of the person again, and walks again 2 times at most. `b438f69cb`
39. In the last Run, the model went between Pallet Town and Route 1 for about 120 decisions (issue #27). The old counts became 0 again at each new cell or new text, so they did not see the loop. Now one stuck counter counts the Decision points since the last progress: a new map, Milestone, exit, level up or text. At 30, and at each 30 more, a reflection turn gives the full Atlas and asks for a lesson and a new objective. Mom healed the party, but the model wrote no note; its notes were guesses and HP values. Now the prompt tells the important events and asks for a note: a fact that the game proved, or a lesson. The model tried the water edge south of Pallet Town 24 times; now the Atlas and the Harness say that only SURF crosses it. The model bot is now version 4. `ff796a107`
40. The panel "Stats" of the Viewer was a list of cards, and the shape of a Run did not show in it (issue #25). A prototype showed 7 layouts on a real Run record of 245 Model calls; the chosen one has lanes on one axis of frames: Milestones, Areas, cost, Stuck counter, Model calls, played actions, Summary calls and battles. A drag or a click on the lanes selects a part of the Run, and the cards under them show the actions chosen, the reasons of the impossible actions and each call with its actions. An Area is a town, a route or another place of the Town Map, from the map sections of the game. In the prototype, a click on one `go D` showed 6 played actions with one impossible `readText`, and the Stuck counter went to 28, near the Reflection turn at 30. `277991348`
41. In the last Run, the model could not buy a POTION at the Poké Mart of Viridian City, then wrote `press(Up)` three times, and the Run stopped (issue #28). Now the Bot also reads an action in the form of a call, as `press(Up)`, `buy(POTION, 2)` or `use(POTION, SQUIRTLE)`. Each `buy` of an item that was not on the first row of the buy list failed: "the cursor does not move from 0". The Harness pressed one time and read the cursor at once. Now the buy list, the shop menu and the Bag list of `sell` use the input of the slow menus: the Harness waits for the release, presses the key until the game reads it, and then reads the cursor. Also, the shop menu (BUY, SELL, SEE YA!) is now one Decision point of the model: it chooses `buy`, `sell` or `leave`, with no `press`, and the code does all the menus of the Mart. The model bot goes to version 5. `use` on a walk now also works with REPEL, SUPER REPEL, MAX REPEL and ESCAPE ROPE: they need no Pokémon. A second `use` on a walk did not open the Bag: the Harness pressed A before the Start menu read the input. After a `buy` or a `sell` that the game refuses, the Harness also goes back to the shop menu. `a742511ec`, `27e6ac9ad`, `d95fe0515`, `718183a3c`, `5768d6fb7`, `158c1c867`
42. In the last Run, the model went two times to the BULBASAUR ball (issue #33). The target of a person showed only "talked", and the letters change at each decision. Now a person target also shows the last text of the person. At the yes/no, the model wrote `press Right` and `press A`, and A chose YES. Now a choice has no `press`: the actions are `choose` and `zoom`, in and out of a battle. At the nickname question, the model answered the question before it. Now the choice line shows the question: `The game asks: "…" Choose YES or NO.` The model bot goes to version 6. YES at the nickname opened the naming screen, and each next action waited 3600 frames there. Now the naming screen is a Decision point: the model gives `name <text>`, and the Harness types the text and presses OK. `name` alone keeps the name. The rule "answer NO to the nickname" goes. The question stays in the choice line after a `zoom`, and the prompt lists the keys that the Harness reads. `7e73dc61a`
43. The Reflection turn came after 30 Decision points with no progress, and the Stuck counter never went past 28 in the last two Runs (spec #34). Now it comes after 20, and after each 20 more. In the last Run, the model wrote `goTo 9, 42` and two more answers with a comma in Viridian Forest, and the Run stopped. Now the Bot reads a comma between the arguments as a space, and the reason of an answer that it cannot read gives the form of the action and one example. The model bot goes to version 7. The panel "Stats" now has a lane of the prompt tokens of each Model call, and a selection shows the smallest and the largest prompt: in the last Run, the prompt went from 1 600 to about 5 100 tokens. On Route 1, the model wrote `press Start` four times to use a POTION with an empty Bag, and each time the Harness waited 3600 frames. Now `press` has no Start, and the Bot cannot read a `press Start` answer: its reason names `use <item>` and the items of the Bag that work now. SQUIRTLE fainted from the poison on a walk, and after "whited out!" `readText` also waited 3600 frames: the text of the warp ("PLOK scurried to a POKéMON CENTER…") waits for A in its own window, not in the message box. Now `readText` reads it, and reads the whiteout to the walk in the Pokémon Center or the house. The blackboard of the school of Viridian City asked "Which topic do you want to read?" with a grid of 6 options, and 2 actions waited 3600 frames there. Now the Harness reads the grid menu of `multichoicegrid` as a choice, and `choose` moves the cursor in the grid.
