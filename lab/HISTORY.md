# History of the lab

The steps of the lab, in order. Each step gives what we added, and what a Run taught us when a Run caused the step. Use it as the plan of a video. Add a step at the end of each piece of work.

## 1. The emulator and the Viewer (2026-10-09 to 10-10)

## 6. The Viewer renewal: spec #54 (2026-10-27)

49. The Viewer gets a design system (issue #55). The Viewer had grown one panel at a time: about 290 lines of CSS inline in the page, and DOM built by hand in each panel. The user chose a direction from a prototype with three of them, "Glass bento" (a dark blue, one periwinkle accent, translucent cards), over "Instrument" (flat and dense, orange) and "Pokédex" (heavy borders and offset shadows), and asked for a second, simpler iteration with only the base components and no glow. The Viewer now uses Preact, Tailwind v4 (compiled by Bun with its plugin, with no build step), Lucide for the icons, and Raleway and JetBrains Mono from Fontsource (ADR 0004). A third tab, Components, shows each component in all its states: type, colors, button, field (text, number with a unit, select, search, error), switch, checkbox, tabs (line and segmented), pill, bar (HP in the colors of the game), number, card and tooltip. Each one has a small motion: a button scales when you press it, a tab indicator slides, a bar leaves a trail when its value falls, a number counts to its new value, a card takes the accent on hover. All motion stops with reduced motion. The navigation bar is the first part of the Viewer that uses them: the name of the lab and the three tabs, with an icon and the sliding indicator. The old panels keep their CSS in a cascade layer below Tailwind, until they move to the components. `AGENTS.md` and the coding standards now tell agents and reviewers to use these components, and to ask the user before they make a new one.
