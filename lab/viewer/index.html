blocked water tall grass ➜ ledge warp (outline: inactive) person (hollow: off the screen) trainer trainer sight active trigger (!) inactive trigger sign hidden item never seen (whole map)
Keys: arrows, X = A, W or Z = B, Enter = Start, ⌫ = Select, Q = L, S = R, G = overlay, M = sound. With the overlay, point at a cell to see its data.

Runs

The Runs of the Bots play at the same time, at full speed, with no sound. To show another Run does not stop them: "Stop the Run" stops the shown Run, with the end user stop and a Run record. Empty, the model and the cost limit take the default model and LAB_COST_LIMIT (or 0.50 $). The reasoning is low by default; off is cheaper, but the model makes more mistakes. The starter is the first Pokémon that the model must choose; with free, it chooses. "continue" starts the Bot from the end of a Run record, with its Memory tree, its Mission and its Atlas; the cost limit counts the new Model calls only, and the model can be another one.

plays the actions of the Bot in the game; decides nothing

Frozen game

Knowledge level

what the Run knows of the world: the maps visited, the cells seen on the screen (15 × 10), the exits taken with their destination, the exits seen, the signs read, the people seen (at the cell where the player last saw them) and their texts, the person of the last talk, and the edges blocked by water. Run record and parallel Runs

          

            A Run of a Bot has a Run record: origin (ROM, seed, save), Bot, keys at each frame, events and result. It goes to
            lab/runs/ at the end of the Run. The keys alone replay the Run: panel "Replay". A Run can continue a stopped Run ("continue" in the panel "Runs"): the Harness replays the keys of the record, keeps its events (Memory tree, Mission, Atlas), and the Bot plays on. The new record has the old keys and events, then the new ones. Its cost limit counts the new Model calls only.
          

          

            The Harness plays 4 Runs at most: the Viewer Run, and 3 Runs of Bots in workers, at full speed and with no sound.
            A Run of a Bot gives the same Run record as alone.

Model bot: prompt and actions

          

            
Model call
at each walk, choice, battle action or move, in the party menu, at the shop menu of a Poké Mart and at the naming screen.

            
Answer
an action, its reason, the Mission (the story step, kept until it is reached) and the Objective (what the model does now) (ADR 0003).

            
Each prompt
the Milestones and the next one (a name and a place only), the Mission, the top nodes of the Memory tree, what the game waits for, the Objective, the stuck counter, the tries since the last progress, the last decisions with their texts, the important events, the party, the money and the Bag. Then the part of the Decision point: at a walk, the extract of the Atlas below.

            
Walk
an extract of the Atlas: the current map and the texts of its people in full, 1 500 characters for the other maps. The map is a grid of all its rows: ? is a cell not seen, a letter is a target (go B).

            
Targets
in groups (exits, unseen cells, people and signs, far maps), each with its distance and direction, and the marks talked (with the last text of the person), read or taken. The extract also lists the edges to other maps. When a person moves during a go, the code finds the person and walks again (2 times at most).

            
viewAtlas
the full Atlas, one time at most at each Decision point. No frames run.

            
Memory tree
the model adds a note after an important event or a lesson. A Summary call merges two nodes of the same size. A node of 2^k notes has 200 × (k + 1) characters at most, with the newest facts first. A summary that is too long ends at its last full sentence. zoom <a-b> opens a node.

            
Choice
one line with the question and the options, for example The game asks: "Do you want to give a nickname to this BULBASAUR?" Choose YES or NO. A zoom or a viewAtlas does not hide the question. The actions are choose <option> and zoom, also in a battle.

            
use
at a walk: a medicine on a Pokémon, or REPEL, SUPER REPEL, MAX REPEL and ESCAPE ROPE alone.

            
Shop menu
the items for sale with their prices, the items of the Bag that the Mart buys, and the money. The actions are buy <item> <quantity>, sell <item> <quantity> and leave. The code does all the menus of the Mart.

            
Naming screen
what the screen names and the maximum length. The actions are name <text>, and name alone to keep the name. The model is free to give a nickname.

            
press
only at a walk, in a battle and in the party menu, never at a choice, the shop menu or the naming screen. It has no Start: the model bot does not know the Start menu. The reason of a press Start answer tells to use <item>, with the items that work now, or that the Bag is empty.

            
Forms of an action
a call is read too: press(Up) is press Up, use(POTION, SQUIRTLE) is use POTION on SQUIRTLE. A comma between the arguments is a space: goTo 9, 42 is goTo 9 42. The commas of choose and name stay in the text.

            
Impossible action
the same conversation goes on with the reason of the Harness and the exits of the map (3 times at most). For an answer that the Bot cannot read, the reason gives the form of the action and one example.

            
Panels
"Model" shows each Model call in full. "Stats" shows the Run as lanes on one axis of frames (Milestones, Areas, cost, Stuck counter, prompt tokens, Model calls, played actions, Summary calls, battles); a drag or a click selects a part of the Run, with the smallest and the largest prompt in tokens. "Memory tree" shows the top nodes with zoom, and the Summary calls.

          

        

        

          
Model bot: progress, reflection and notes

          

            
Stuck counter
the Decision points since the last progress (harness.noProgress.stuck). Each Macro-action or Raw input that ends at a Decision point adds 1, also when it is impossible, and also in a battle. Progress makes it 0 again. The prompt shows it at each call; noProgressLimit ends a Run on it.

            
Progress
a new map, a new Milestone, a new exit in the Atlas (a warp or an edge seen for the first time), a level up, or a new text of a person. A text heard before from the same person and a new cell are not progress.

            
Reflection turn
the first walk at 20 on the stuck counter, then at 40, 60… A normal Model call, with the full Atlas after the extract and an instruction: read the Memory tree and the Atlas, zoom freely, write a lesson in note (what failed, why, what next) and set a new objective. No viewAtlas there.

            
Events that ask for a note
since the last Model call: a new map, a lost battle, a heal (all HP full again with no battle and no item use; the line names the map and the last person talked to), a new item, 2 impossible actions or more in a row. One line "Something important happened: …" each, then "Write a note if it can help you later." The model decides.

            
Notes
a fact that the game proved, or a lesson: what works, what fails and why. Never a guess, never HP values.

            
Blocked edge
an edge with water between the two maps: only SURF crosses it. The extract and the full Atlas say "you cannot go there now" with the reason, the edge is not a target, and a goTo past it gives the same reason. A goTo past an edge cell that leads to a blocked cell of the next map is impossible too: it tells to cross at another cell.

            
Run record
each Model call has stuck and reflection. The panel "Model" shows them in the list and in the title of a call.
